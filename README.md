### About

I design and build embedded systems for regulated, real-world environments — from medical imaging devices to edge AI platforms. My focus is on production readiness: systems that meet compliance requirements, scale across product lines, and remain maintainable as they evolve.

### What I work on

- 🏥 **Medical imaging devices** — Colonoscopes, ureteroscopes, and stereoscopes built on embedded Linux, designed for EMI/EMC compliance and long-term maintainability
- ⚡ **Edge AI pipelines** — Real-time inference on NVIDIA Jetson (Xavier, Orin) and Raspberry Pi for medical device intelligence
- 🔧 **Full-stack embedded** — Hardware schematics in Altium, custom BSPs, Linux device drivers, and firmware architectures
- 📐 **Modular platforms** — A reusable architecture approach that brought product development from 8 months down to 2
